## Traceability

§48 makes this block mandatory: an ADR that cannot be traced back to the
specification is a decision nobody can audit later.

## Context

What was true when the decision was taken, and what forced a decision at all.
Constraints belong here, not in the decision.

## Decision

What was decided, stated so a reader can tell whether the code follows it.

## Consequences

What this buys, and what it costs. An ADR with no costs listed has not been
thought through.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. Rejecting an option for a bad
reason is fine to record; hiding that it was considered is not.

## How this is enforced

Where the decision is checked mechanically — a test, a constraint, a lint rule.
A decision nothing enforces is a preference.
